About

I am an AI Engineer who builds AI systems people can trust. For twelve years I have shipped production AI across finance, government, and climate tech, from LLM platforms and RAG pipelines to computer vision. My research focus is digital twins for district cooling: at Aarhus University I work on locating faults in cooling networks where sensors are sparse, on testing the fault monitors a digital twin relies on, and on bringing building models into digital twin platforms. It is the same question that drives my work on AI Safety: can you trust what the machine says is real?

Digital Twins for District Cooling (SWiM)

PhD Research, Aarhus University

District cooling keeps buildings such as hospitals and data centres running in hot, dense cities, and small faults can waste energy for a long time before anyone notices.

Result In progress since August 2026.

How It Was Built

PhD Fellow in the Software Engineering and Computing Systems section, supervised by Peter Gorm Larsen and Carl Peter Leslie Schultz. Part of SWiM, Sustainable Water-based Cooling in Megacities, a five-year project funded by the Grundfos Foundation with partners in Denmark and Singapore.

  • Start each digital twin from the building model (BIM) of the installation.
  • Run the twins on the Digital Twin as a Service platform.
  • Keep AI support verifiable, so operators can rely on what it tells them.
