Privacy Policy

This page covers the Pantheon, the set of personal apps Jady Pamella runs on her own server, and in particular the Google app named Pantheon that connects to Google Drive.

Who Uses It

The Pantheon is a private system. Its only user is Jady Pamella. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What Google Data It Uses

The Pantheon app asks for access to Google Drive in Jady's own Google account. It uses that access for one purpose: to store and check her encrypted backups in a folder of her Drive, and to restore them when needed. It does not read, change or delete any other file.

How the Data Is Kept

Backups are encrypted on her server before they reach Google Drive, so Google stores only encrypted data. The access token stays on her server, readable by her account alone. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or model training.

How to Remove Access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account settings, under Security, Third-party apps with account access. Removing it stops the backups from reaching Google Drive and deletes nothing.
