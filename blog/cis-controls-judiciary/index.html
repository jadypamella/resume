Cybersecurity in the judiciary: extending the CIS Controls

A peer-reviewed study in Elsevier Computers & Security proposes an expanded CIS cybersecurity model for digital protection across the Brazilian judiciary, with the controls assessed published as an open dataset.

By Jady Pamella Published in October 2025 Computers & Security, volume 157

What does the study propose?

The study proposes an expanded version of the CIS cybersecurity model for the Brazilian judiciary, adding controls to the CIS framework for the digital protection of courts. It was co-authored at LabRisk, the cybersecurity and risk lab of the University of Brasília.
